# Refund-recovery policy: independent test failed

The proposed recovery policy targeted customers with observed refunds when a merchant's latest mature orders indicated elevated return risk. It used no future returns or hidden customer traits. Three fresh Gemini planner/reviewer trials selected the policy from development evidence; the deterministic executor applied the recorded program.

On 80 independent confirmation markets (16 seeds × five scenarios), after 400 shopping days with campaigns stopped at day 100, modeled platform contribution was **−$0.834875 per market before model cost**; the seed-clustered 95% interval was **[−$1.631378, −$0.059622]**. Merchant contribution averaged +$4.414875 with an interval spanning zero. These results do not support promotion.

Fresh approvals cost $0.076854 in total. They did not demonstrate superior marketing economics. The frozen closed-market simulator, costs, customer responses and all negative outcomes are retained. See `protocol.json`, `development.json`, `evaluation.json` and `agent-choices.json` for the complete evidence.
