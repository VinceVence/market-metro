# Intent and conversion team: live demonstration

The analyst, scientist and commerce manager made 6 fresh Gemini decisions. The scientist selected a forest, a 28-day historical window, latest-product intent, and adaptive funding feedback. One real local fit ran, followed by 58 local response updates and 2 automatic publications. At the second review the team retained the model while its response estimates continued updating.

Customers kept trading during both reviews: 20 and 24 checkouts occurred before publication. The economy and original fixed controller stayed unchanged. This demonstrates autonomous operation and useful observed-market adaptation, not a general profit guarantee.

The first two rows compare forecasts on the same issued contacts, recorded before their outcomes. They are not a counterfactual policy-profit test. The financial rows compare the live team with strong fixed rules under identical initial funds, opportunities, consent and income. Merchant contribution fell; the economic guard remains unpassed.

## What the team now owns

- Analyst: distinguish searches, contact responses, approved funding and mature retained purchases. The input carries explicit definitions and denominators.
- Scientist: choose forest/logistic, a historical window, intent scope and either historical scoring or adaptive funding adjustment. Train and validate a real local model.
- Commerce manager: publish or retain audience, contact type, capacity, value threshold and fulfillment/settlement controls.
- Trusted execution: enforce consent, budgets, complete funded baskets, merchant margin floors and idempotent payment actions. Scores and payments are local tools; they are not additional LLM agents.

The funding adjustment uses a measured historical prior with eight effective contacts and prior-day observations from a 28-day rolling window. It shifts recommendation odds as market response changes. This is a modeling assumption, not a causal estimate. The inquiry estimator already updates separately, so its observations are not counted a second time in that adjustment. Every daily fit is retained in warehouse events; weekly checkpoints keep the office feed readable.

## PayPal and cost

Authorized funds, captures, delivery and refund outcomes give the model its commerce feedback. The native Sandbox sampler remains available through the existing saved-buyer integration. This registered run kept it off: **zero native payment calls**. All payment amounts in this run are simulated and no real money moved.

Provider usage cost: **$0.011666**, a token-metered estimate, within the $0.25 run cap. Lifetime recorded usage: $3.622899. Local fitting and the 58 feedback updates used CPU and made no model API calls. No APAC changes or development subagents.
