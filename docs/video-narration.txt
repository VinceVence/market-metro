A normal day for Leo
People walk in. They look around. Then… they leave. For Leo, it's becoming a familiar story. He runs a furniture shop in a busy neighborhood. The products are ready. The customers are out there. But after days of trying to bring them back, he's still guessing. Who should he reach? And what should he offer? The clues are already there, in what shoppers browse, what they ask about, and what they buy. Leo just needs a way to connect them.

What Market Metro is
Could AI help him figure it out, and bring those customers back? Meet Market Metro: a simulated commerce world where AI agents learn from shopper behavior, build campaigns, and show what happens next.

The right data for the question
First, we give the agent a map. That's our ontology. It connects shoppers, products, interactions, and payments into one understandable story. It keeps a product recommendation separate from a general inquiry, and makes sure one purchase isn't counted twice. Now the agent has a clearer picture of what happened, which examples to learn from, and who might be ready to return.

From understanding to action
It studies demand, checks available stock, and uses local tools to train two models.

Test before sending
Before a campaign goes out, the verifier checks the candidates against fixed rules. Which one produces better outcomes for merchants and PayPal?

The recommendation becomes a purchase
Then we follow the campaign into the market. Day thirty-five. A shopper who hasn't purchased in sixteen days receives a recommendation. A ninety-six-dollar side table catches their interest. They buy it. The next day, it arrives. Under fixed rules, that same request went unfilled.

A payment you can trace
Here's where PayPal closes the loop. In our demo, that exact order connects to a PayPal Sandbox capture. We can follow the purchase through the payment, the processing fee, and what the merchant receives.

What changed
And across this market, the difference adds up. Sixteen more retained purchases. Over twenty-three hundred dollars in additional payment volume. More than a thousand dollars in extra merchant contribution. And roughly a hundred fifteen dollars more in modeled PayPal net, after AI cost.
