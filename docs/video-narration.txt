A normal day for Leo
It was a normal day for Leo. His shop was ready, but customers kept walking. For days, he faced one question: who should he reach, and what should he offer?

What Market Metro is
Could AI do this for Leo? Meet Market Metro, a simulated commerce world where AI agents analyze behavior, train models, test campaigns and measure what works.

The right data for the question
The ontology gives data meaning. It connects shoppers, products and payments, and shows the agent which history and signals it can trust.

From understanding to action
The agent spots the opportunity, chooses twenty eight days of history, and trains two models. Every decision leaves a checkpoint.

Test before sending
Before anything launches, the verifier tests both models in matching markets and selects the stronger result for merchants and PayPal.

The recommendation becomes a purchase
Then it happens: a shopper returns. They buy the ninety six dollar table, and it arrives the next day. The same request went unfilled under fixed rules.

A payment you can trace
PayPal Sandbox captures that exact order and sends the receipt back to the warehouse. The payment, fee and merchant proceeds all connect to the same purchase.

What changed
The same market delivers sixteen more purchases kept, extra payment volume, and higher merchant contribution and modeled PayPal net, even after AI cost. Every gain is measured against the same fixed rules.

Follow the full trail
AG Grid makes the entire journey inspectable: the models, the agent's decisions, the payments, and the results. Open any step and see the evidence.
