A normal day for Leo
It was a normal day for Leo. His shop was ready, but customers kept walking. For days, he faced the same question: who should he reach, and what should he offer? The clues were there, scattered across shopper activity, product interest and payments.

What Market Metro is
Could AI do this for Leo? Meet Market Metro: a simulated commerce environment where AI agents analyze shopper behavior, train models and test marketing campaigns. We measure how their decisions change purchases, merchant contribution and PayPal payment outcomes.

The right data for the question
The ontology gives that data meaning. It connects shoppers, products, contacts and payments, then maps each prediction to the right training examples. Recommendations and inquiries stay separate. Earlier signals can be inputs; later outcomes are reserved for labels and evaluation.

From understanding to action
The agent studies demand and available stock, chooses twenty eight days of history, and asks local tools to train two models. Its decision summary, tool calls and results become checkpoints you can inspect.

Test before sending
Before a campaign launches, the verifier tests the candidate models in matching development markets. It checks PayPal and merchant outcomes, then publishes an eligible tested configuration or keeps the current policy.

The recommendation becomes a purchase
On day thirty five, a recommendation reaches our quiet shopper. They buy a ninety six dollar side table, and it arrives the next day. Fixed rules left that same request unfilled.

A payment you can trace
PayPal Sandbox captures that exact order. Its receipt records ninety six dollars gross, the provider fee and merchant proceeds. Those records link back to the warehouse, so the proof is traceable without counting another sale.

What changed
Across the same market, sixteen more purchases were completed and kept. Payment volume grows by two thousand three hundred forty-five dollars. Merchant contribution and modeled PayPal net both rise, after AI cost. The numbers on screen compare against the same fixed rules.

Follow the full trail
AG Grid lets judges inspect the entire journey: trained models, agent decisions, campaign outcomes and the native PayPal receipt. Open a checkpoint and follow the evidence behind the result.
