A normal day for Leo
They look. They leave. Leo wonders why. Meet Leo, a furniture shop owner. Every day, people browse and walk away. He has clues: what they looked at, almost bought, and paid for. But those clues sit in different places. His next offer is still a guess. Who should he reach, and with what?

What Market Metro is
Could AI help bring those customers back? Meet Market Metro: a simulated commerce environment where agents observe shoppers, train models, and test campaigns. Then we measure whether those decisions actually help.

The right data for the question
It starts by connecting the clues. Our ontology connects shoppers, products, contacts and payments, so each prediction uses the right examples. Recommendations stay separate from inquiries. One rule: past signals become inputs; later outcomes become labels. The model can't peek into the future.

From understanding to action
With that map, the agent studies demand and stock, selects twenty eight days of history, and asks local tools to train two models. Every choice is saved in a checkpoint.

Test before sending
A prediction isn't enough. The verifier tests both candidates, checking PayPal and merchant outcomes under matching conditions. The winner earns its place through measured results; otherwise, the current policy stays.

The recommendation becomes a purchase
Now follow one recommendation. On day thirty five, it reaches a shopper who's been quiet for sixteen days. They buy a ninety six dollar side table. It arrives tomorrow. Under fixed rules, that request went unfilled.

A payment you can trace
That purchase connects to PayPal Sandbox. It captures the same ninety six dollar order, showing the fee and merchant proceeds. Each record links back to the warehouse, so one purchase is counted once.

What changed
Across the same market: sixteen more purchases kept. Two thousand three hundred forty-five dollars more payment volume. Over a thousand dollars more merchant contribution. And about a hundred fifteen dollars more modeled PayPal net, after AI cost. All compared with fixed rules.

Agentic commerce that learns
