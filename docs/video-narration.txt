A normal day for Leo
It was a normal day for Leo. He opened his furniture shop, straightened the display, and waited. People hurried past. One checked their phone. Another glanced at the window and kept walking. Days went by, but the shop stayed quiet. Leo had data everywhere, yet he still couldn't tell who to reach or what to offer.

What Market Metro is
Could AI do this for Leo? Meet Market Metro: a simulated commerce environment where AI agents analyze shopper behavior, train models, and test marketing campaigns. We measure how those decisions change purchases, merchant contribution, and PayPal payments.

The right data for the question
An ontology is the shared map underneath. It connects shoppers, products and payments, routes recommendations and inquiries to the right training data, and keeps later outcomes out of model inputs.

From understanding to action
Now follow one saved reactivation campaign. The agent analyzes demand, chooses twenty eight days of history, and fits two models. A verifier tests the campaign before it reaches shoppers.

Test before sending
The first idea doesn't get a free pass. A verifier tests both rankings in separate development markets and selects the one that earns more while protecting merchants.

The recommendation becomes a purchase
On day thirty five, the selected recommendation reaches our shopper. They buy a ninety six dollar side table, and it arrives the next day; fixed rules left that same request unfilled.

A payment you can trace
We mirror that order once through PayPal Sandbox. The completed capture records the fee and merchant proceeds, then returns a receipt that links all the way back to the purchase.

What changed
Against fixed rules, sixteen more purchases were completed and kept. That's two thousand three hundred forty-five dollars in extra payment volume, over a thousand dollars in merchant contribution, and a hundred fifteen dollars in modeled PayPal net after AI cost.

Follow the full trail
Judges can follow the whole trail in AG Grid, from model training and the agent's decision to the PayPal receipt, instead of taking the result on trust.

Agentic commerce that learns
Market Metro turns observed activity into tested campaigns and traceable payments: agentic commerce that learns.
