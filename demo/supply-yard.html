ASSUMPTIONS AND AUTHORITY

What PayPal does here.

The simulated PayPal decision is a merchant reserve review. The team finds delivery evidence, challenges mismatched claims, and recommends how much merchant money can be released. You authorize that local decision. Released cash changes the next shift’s purchasing power.

The saved receipt supplies a previously verified payment amount, fee, net and capture reference. It is replayed once in the first shift’s Mara Paper order. Subsequent orders are generated. There is no new checkout, capture, refund or reserve call to PayPal on this page. The proof is cached at the displayed verification time, not re-verified each run.

Authored demo rules

  • Each merchant starts with $50 of synthetic working capital. Kits sell for $10; procurement costs $4, $5.60 and $4.80 across three shifts. Supplier stocks and delivery draws are generated. Suppliers allocate stock in a fixed queue.
  • 100% of each new order’s net proceeds enters a simulated review hold. This is a teaching assumption, not PayPal’s default reserve policy. Generated payments use an assumed 3% fee. Only the linked receipt uses its recorded PayPal sandbox fee.
  • Reserve target = 100% of unverified gross + 20% of verified gross, capped at that order’s net. The initial-feed and investigated options use the same formula. Prior-shift reserves remain held; there is no modeled timed expiry.
  • A matching carrier record needs both the order ID and the individual kit ID. A record for another order is rejected. An office claim without matching carrier proof remains unresolved for human review.
  • The scripted investigators receive source records, not hidden delivery outcomes. The environment generates missing scans as deliveries occur. Mara Paper’s first order is an authored partial-delivery case; Jun Print’s first order has an actual failure and wrong-order proof.
  • After review, the synthetic refund window closes. All actually undelivered units receive $10 refunds from current held proceeds first, then merchant cash, then prior reserves. Any remaining shortfall is assumed to be covered by PayPal. This is not a statement of actual PayPal coverage policy.
  • Only the selected policy is executed. Proposals are not measured alternative outcomes. No merchant churn, legal decision, calibrated fraud model, operating costs, production ROI or LLM advantage is claimed.

The original optimization experiment with editable fees and churn remains available at Fixed vs agents. Optional new-payment testing is separate at PayPal sandbox.
