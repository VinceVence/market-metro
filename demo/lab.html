    PayPal’s role

    The proposed user is a PayPal merchant-reserve reviewer. Their decision is whether to reduce an existing minimum reserve, retain it, or request more evidence. The reserve adjustment is simulated here; no public reserve-override endpoint was identified. The future Sandbox adapter demonstrates the payment consequences, not reserve authority.

    What the agents contribute

    They decide which incomplete or conflicting evidence to investigate, which counterfactual distinguishes cash from capacity, and whether the recommendation survives adverse assumptions. In this first walkthrough those roles are scripted. A structured optimizer remains a required baseline.

    What would count as value

    Better reviewer decisions or less review effort without weakening predefined financial constraints. Evaluate unrecovered losses, payment contribution margin, and reviewer time using representative data. Merchant sales and reserve releases are not PayPal revenue or savings.

    Novelty claim

    The completed public research did not identify this exact reserve-and-recovery investigation loop. PayPal already has agent, simulation, and policy research; internal absence and competitive advantage are unverified.
